# Game Design

Case: The Midnight Gallery

## Pillars
- Deterministic truth with replayable interrogation.
- Limited actions to force prioritization.
- Contradiction hunting through evidence-backed questioning.

## Content Scope
- 5 suspects
- 5 locations
- 13 clues
- 5 contradictions
- 3 red herrings
- 4 endings

## Canonical Outcome
- Attacker: Celeste Vale
- Thief: Marina Crowe
- Motive: protect family debt secrets and force insurance leverage

The canonical values are only stored in backend seed data and case.canonical_answer.
